url for dataset: 

Dataset contents:

Object - name
Close-Approach (CA) Date - In terms of Gregorian Calendar
CA Distance Nominal (LD | au) - average distance of object to Earth
CA Distance Minimum (LD | au) - minimum distance of object to Earth
V relative (km/s) - velocity wrt Earth
V infinity (km/s) - velocity wrt massless Earth
H (absolute magnitude)
Diameter (metres)

distances measured from centre of object to centre of Earth
LD - Lunar distance, AU - Astronomical Units
